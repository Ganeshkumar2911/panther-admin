Tab Navigation Test

Test the two ways to open in a new tab:

1. Normal Click (Single Click):

✅ Bina Ctrl dabaye sirf click karein: Chrome turant new tab khol kar uss tab par switch / navigate kar deta hai.

2. With Ctrl + Shift:

⚠️ Chrome ka Native Rule:
• Ctrl + Click = Chrome background me rakhta hai.
• Ctrl + Shift + Click = Chrome new active tab me switch karta hai.
• Single Click = Direct new tab me switch karta hai!
